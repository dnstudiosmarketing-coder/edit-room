Privacy policy

How the DN Studios team app handles your information. Last updated 23 October 2026.

Who runs the app

DN Studios runs this app for its own team: video editors, media buyers, supervisors and admins. It isn't open to the public: only people an admin adds can sign in.

What the app keeps

  • Your account: your name, username and profile picture, when you last used the app, records of your sign-ins that keep your account safe, and your password, stored only in scrambled (hashed) form so nobody can read it.
  • Your work: the briefs you log, their stages, deadlines, notes and video links, when you submitted them, and what you earned for them, including bonuses. When an admin records a payment to you in the app, a record of that payment: how much, when, who recorded it, any note they added, how many videos and bonuses it covered, and, if the amount was corrected later, what it was first recorded as.
  • Scripts: when an admin sends a script through the app, who it's for, its name, file name, size and category, when it was sent, and whether the editor took it on or couldn't (with the reason they gave). The script file itself is kept in DN Studios' Google Drive, not in the app's database.
  • Payment details: the name on your bank account, your phone number, your bank and your account number. Earlier details are kept when you change them, so an admin can see when the account your pay goes to has changed.
  • Notifications: if you allow phone notifications, the address your browser gives the app for sending them, and a record of each notification sent.
  • Google Drive uploads and scripts: described under Google Drive below.

Who can see it

Admins can see your work, earnings, payments and payment details, so they can hand out work, check videos and pay people. Other editors see your name, your picture and how many videos you've submitted on time, on the leaderboard. A video an admin picks as Video of the moment shows to the whole team with your name.

Media buyers see the finished videos admins approve, with the name of the editor who made each one. Supervisors see the same, and a list of every video submitted each day, with its name, category, the editor's name, the time it came in and whether it has been approved. Neither sees briefs in progress, scripts, earnings, payment details or the leaderboard. A script can be opened only by admins and, once they take it on, by the editor it was sent to.

DN Studios doesn't sell your information or show you ads, and the app has no tracking or analytics.

Where it's stored

The app's records are kept with Supabase, the database service the app runs on, and its pages are served by GitHub Pages. Phone notifications travel through your browser maker's push service, such as Google's or Apple's, encrypted so that service can't read them. Videos uploaded through the app, and scripts sent through it, are stored in DN Studios' Google Drive.

Google Drive

An admin can connect DN Studios' own Google Drive so editors can upload finished videos straight into it, and admins can send scripts through the app. When they connect it, the app asks Google for the account's email address and for permission to work only with the Drive files and folders it's given (Google calls this permission drive.file): the folders an admin picks in Google's folder picker, the videos the app uploads there, and a folder it makes for scripts (DN Studios scripts) with the scripts admins send. It can't open, change or delete anything else in that Drive.

With that permission, the app:

  • keeps the connected account's email address and the names of the folders an admin picked;
  • sends each video from the editor's phone or computer straight to the chosen folder, so videos never pass through or stay on DN Studios' servers;
  • names each file after the brief and the editor, checks it arrived whole, and shares it so anyone with its link can watch it, which is how admins open submitted videos;
  • keeps a record of each upload: the file's name, size, type and link, and when it finished;
  • saves each script an admin sends in the DN Studios scripts folder, and fetches it from there when an admin, or the editor who took it on, opens it, keeping only the file's name, size, type and Drive id.

The key Google gives the app to keep the connection open stays on the app's server, out of reach of the web, and is used only for these uploads and scripts. Information the app gets from Google is used only to run them. It isn't sold, used for advertising or passed to anyone else.

DN Studios' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

An admin can disconnect Google Drive in the app's Settings at any time, which ends the app's access with Google. The Google account's owner can also remove it in their Google Account. Videos and scripts already there stay in the Drive.

On your phone or computer

The app keeps a few things in your browser so it works smoothly: your sign-in, your Day or Night choice, the screen and Drive folder you last used, when it last asked about notifications, the team's profile pictures so they don't download at every visit, an unfinished upload so it can carry on after an interruption, and, for media buyers and supervisors, when they last looked at the finished videos, so new ones can be marked. A script you open, or a video you download, is saved on your device like any other download. Signing out clears your sign-in and the pictures. It also saves its own files so it can open without a connection. It doesn't use cookies.

How long it's kept

Records stay for as long as DN Studios uses the app, so its history of work and pay stays complete. When a login is switched off it stops working, and the work done under it stays in the studio's records.

Your choices

You can change your picture and payment details in the app at any time, and turn phone notifications off in the app or in your phone's settings. To see, correct or remove anything else the app holds about you, or to ask about this policy, speak to a DN Studios admin.

Changes to this policy
